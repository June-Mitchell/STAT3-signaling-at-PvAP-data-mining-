STAT3 Signaling at Astrocytic Perivascular Endfeet: Transcriptomic and Proteomic Data Mining

This repository contains two complementary Jupyter notebooks developed to investigate the local expression, compartmentalization, and regulation of STAT3 signaling pathways in astrocytic perivascular processes (PvAPs) using publicly available transcriptomic and proteomic datasets. These analyses were performed in the context of studying canonical and non-canonical STAT3 signaling at the gliovascular unit during neuroinflammatory conditions, with a particular focus on astrocyte endfeet specialization.

The repository includes two workflows:

1. Transcriptomic Analysis – Cohen-Salmon Dataset
(Data mining Transcriptomics Cohen-Salmon analysis.ipynb)

This notebook investigates astrocyte and astrocytic endfoot-enriched transcriptomic datasets to determine whether components of the STAT3 signaling pathway are locally expressed and translated at astrocytic PvAPs. The workflow compares total astrocyte versus endfoot RNA profiles and integrates a manually curated list of STAT3-related genes, including canonical and non-canonical signaling intermediates, receptors, transcriptional effectors, and regulatory proteins.

2. Proteomic Analysis – Hill Dataset
(Data mining Proteomics Hills analysis.ipynb)

This notebook analyzes TurboID proximity-labeling proteomics datasets to characterize the protein composition of astrocytic endfeet under both physiological and inflammatory conditions. Comparisons include total astrocytes versus astrocytic endfeet, as well as vehicle-treated and LPS-treated mice, to explore inflammation-induced changes in STAT3 localization and signaling.
The workflow also incorporates CellTalkDB ligand–receptor interaction datasets and curated lists of STAT3-associated receptors and signaling proteins to investigate potential mechanisms of endothelial–astrocyte communication at the gliovascular interface.

Outputs include:

Differential enrichment analyses
Heatmaps and comparative visualizations
Protein and transcript cross-validation
Candidate signaling pathways involved in local STAT3 activation
